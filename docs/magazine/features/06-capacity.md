Most of us plan days that could not fit in a week. The research calls it the planning fallacy, and it offers a modest remedy: breaking work into parts and estimating each gives longer and more accurate totals.

In 3.0, any task can carry an optional estimate. Type `~45m`, `~2h`, or `~1h30m` in capture, or pick from ~15m to ~3h in the Estimate property. When at least one planned task has an estimate, Today's header adds a single line: "About 3 h planned · 6 h available."

The available figure is yours. Settings → Planning asks for your "Hours available on a typical day", with the gentle clarification "For focused work, not your whole day." The same group lets you switch the line off entirely.

The settings copy makes the promise explicit: "An honest look at how much is planned for today, next to the time you have. It never stops you adding more." There is no warning colour and no refusal. The line is there to inform your judgment, not to replace it.
