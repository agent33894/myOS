In a study of about 12,000 work-diary entries, small steps forward on meaningful work were the strongest driver of good days. The behavioral research rates that progress principle as moderate evidence, and draws a careful conclusion from it: make finished work visible and meaningful, not gamified.

**What moved** is that conclusion as a page. It shows one week, Monday to Sunday. **Finished** lists the tasks you completed, grouped by project. **Written** lists the notes you wrote or edited, each marked with its day. **From your journal** gathers a few lines from each day's page. Previous week and Next week step through time.

There is no chart and no score, and there is deliberately no comparison between weeks. A week is not a race against the one before it. The empty states hold to the same spirit: "This week is just getting going." For a past week with nothing recorded: "A quiet week on paper. Nothing was finished or written here that week. Rest and thinking count too."

Everything on the page is computed on demand from your files. Nothing is tallied or stored.
