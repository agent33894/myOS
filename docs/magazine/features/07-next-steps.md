The behavioral research ranked these two among its biggest wins, because the evidence is strong.

**Next step.** Writing a specific plan removed intrusive thoughts about unfinished goals in lab studies by Masicampo and Baumeister (2011). Capture alone is not enough; capture plus a next step is what quiets the mind. Every project now has a **Next step** property, "The very next thing to do". It shows on the project card, on the project page, and in Today under **Next steps**, where **Make task** turns it into a task in today's plan.

**When.** Implementation intentions, plans in an "if this, then that" form, produced an average effect of d = .65 across 94 tests, and between .27 and .66 in a 2024 update of 642. A task can now hold an optional **When** cue in your own words, "after standup, at the café…", shown quietly under its title.

The hint beneath the field is the guardrail in one sentence: "A cue for when or where you'll do it. There are no reminders." Early research on cues suggests that "after X" anchors build habits better than timed reminders. There is no "you missed it" message, ever.
