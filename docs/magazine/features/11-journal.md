A daily note is one of the most common habits among people who keep their notes in files. The market research found it in Obsidian's core plugins, in the Calendar plugin's 3.1 million downloads, and at the centre of Reflect, Logseq, and NotePlan. myOS 3.0 adds its own, as one page per day at `journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

The page is headed "A page for each day. Write as much or as little as you like." Today's entry waits with "What's on your mind today?", and the file is written only when you type the first key. Close the day and the Weekly review add their lines to the same page, under Evening and Weekly review.

A small month strip marks the days that have a page with a single dot. There are no connecting lines and no counts, because the guardrails rule out chains of any kind. Journal pages stay out of the Notes list unless you choose Journal in the Kind filter.

The research on expressive writing finds a small but real benefit, and the guardrails ask for evidence-honest copy. So the Journal promises nothing. It is simply a private place to write.
