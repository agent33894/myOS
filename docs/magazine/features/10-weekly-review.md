Researchers have found that goal-directed behaviour rises after temporal landmarks, such as a new week or month. The effect is moderate and the evidence mostly correlational, but it points at a humane idea: offer a clean slate at the moments people already feel one. The persona walkthroughs found myOS had no weekly review at all.

The 3.0 review follows the familiar Getting Things Done sequence in six steps. Start with the Inbox. Give carried-over tasks a new home. Answer "What's next for these projects?" for any project without a next step. Decide about projects quiet for three weeks: "That's fine. Keep them, park them in Someday, or call them done." Glance at Someday, where "nothing to do here unless something feels ready". Then, if you like, write a few words for today's journal page, under Weekly review.

It ends on "Ready for a fresh week." and a summary of what moved, with a link to **See what moved**.

On your chosen day a small "Weekly review" item appears in the sidebar. It stays until you finish the review or choose **Not this week**, and a switch in Settings hides it for good. It is never a badge and never a count.
