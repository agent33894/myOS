Templater is the second most downloaded plugin for Obsidian, at 5.7 million. The market research read that as demand for "Obsidian without the plugin tax": the most-wanted plugin behaviours shipped as small, built-in defaults. Templates are one of them.

A template is an ordinary note stored in `templates/`, never shown in the Notes list. **New from template…** opens a picker with a live preview, a Title field, and **Create note**: "Dates and the title fill in for you. Everything else is yours to write." `{{date}}`, `{{time}}`, and `{{title}}` expand when you use one.

There are four starters, added only when you ask with **Add starter templates**: Meeting notes, Lecture notes, Weekly plan, and Project brief. They hold headings only. That is a pointed choice. In 1.x, new projects wrote the literal placeholder "[What are you trying to change?]" into people's files, and the guardrails now forbid placeholder text in files outright.

Any page can become a pattern. **Save as template** in its ⋯ menu keeps its text for reuse, and templates can be edited or deleted in Settings.
