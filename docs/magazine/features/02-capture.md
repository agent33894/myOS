The persona walkthroughs found capture quietly losing words. "Soccer practice pickup every tuesday" became a one-time task called "Soccer practice pickup every". "Sam dentist on friday" became "Sam dentist on". A short weekday in the middle of a sentence was not recognized at all.

The 3.0 parser keeps meaning. Full weekday names count anywhere in the line. Short ones like `fri` count after "on", "by", or "next", before a time, or at the end. When a date is lifted out, the connector word left stranded in front of it goes too. `next week` now means next Monday everywhere, in capture and in every date menu. `~30m` or `~1h30m` sets an estimate, and `every tue` sets a repeat.

As you type `@` or `#`, suggestions appear from your projects and tags. An `@name` that matches nothing becomes a button: Create project "Name".

Beneath the field, a line tells you where the thought will land: Inbox, Today, Tasks · Anytime, or a project and a day. It never names a place that does not exist. The walkthrough rated the old promise of a missing "Tasks" page a severity-4 problem, the highest on its scale. Now there is a Tasks page, and a destination you can trust.
