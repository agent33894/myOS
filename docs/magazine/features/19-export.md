Your notes are plain Markdown, which is perfect for you and not always perfect for the person you are sending them to. The market research listed export and sharing among the table-stakes gaps, pointing to Bear's seven export formats as a praised strength.

A page's ⋯ menu now offers four ways out. **Export as PDF…** and **Export as HTML…** open the native save dialog. The HTML is a single self-contained file, with images inlined and the bundled fonts embedded, so it reads the same on any machine. **Copy as rich text** pastes cleanly into mail and documents, and **Copy as Markdown** gives you the source.

Exports are always rendered in the light theme, with the app's own reading styles. Charts stay crisp as SVG. The PDF picks its paper size from where you are, Letter in the United States and a few other countries and A4 elsewhere, and a toast offers **Show in folder** when it is done.

Nothing is uploaded. Export writes a file where you choose, and only where you choose.
