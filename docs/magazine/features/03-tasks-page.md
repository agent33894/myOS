In 2.0, a task with no date, no flag, and no project showed up nowhere. Today lists only what is due, flagged, or in progress, and there was no page listing every task. A household organizer typed "Pay water bill !high" in the walkthrough and never saw it again, except through search. The research rated this the most severe problem it found, because tasks that go in and cannot be found break trust in the whole system. It cites Bellotti and colleagues (CHI '04): people manage tasks well when those tasks stay visible.

The new **Tasks** page, with the subtitle "Tasks without a date, later tasks, and Someday", gives every open task a visible home. **Anytime** holds tasks without a date. **Upcoming** holds tasks dated after this week. **Someday** holds what you have parked on purpose. You can group the list by Project or by Area.

Someday is a real decision, not a failure. The behavioral research notes that choosing to park something closes an open loop much as a plan does. Someday tasks stay out of Today and out of its count, and they come back with one click: Bring back from Someday.
