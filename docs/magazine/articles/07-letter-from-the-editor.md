# Letter from the Editor

*Issue 03 · Autumn 2026. On calm as a discipline, and the season ahead.*

By the myOS Editors

---

Some issues begin with a trend. This one began with a feeling.

"Something feels off with the app and I'm not sure what it is." That was the whole brief, and it is the most honest sentence in this magazine. Anyone who makes things knows it. The work is correct and still not right, and the gap between the two is where most of the craft lives.

In these pages you will read how myOS found that gap and closed it. Our cover story follows the overhaul from unease to diagnosis: a font that never loaded, labels too small and too faint to read comfortably, a notes app dressed up as a newspaper, a filing cabinet that asked you to choose a type and a domain before you had finished a thought. You will see the new silhouette, built on one typeface, warm neutrals, soft depth, a single accent, and one spring. You will find the five decisions that held it together, and a tour of the lining: the revision on every write, the one table, the Markdown writer that leaves your words where you put them.

> Calm is not an absence of features. It is a discipline about which ones deserve your attention.

Then we turn the page to the season ahead.

myOS 3.0 brings twenty features, and they were not chosen by instinct. They came from three research tracks: the market, walkthroughs of the app as three very different people, and the behavioral science of planning, memory, and motivation. Our essays on the market and on the psychology of calm tell you what that research found. "How We Chose Twenty" tells you what it cost to say no.

The features section that follows is organized as the plan is: tasks and planning, rituals and reflection, notes and knowledge, and files and trust. Repeating tasks that record an honest "done 9 of the last 10 times". A morning *Plan my day* and an evening *Close the day*. A journal, a weekly review, links that help, spaced recall of your own notes. Version history that keeps your folder clean.

Every one of them answers to eight written guardrails. No streaks. No guilt. Nudges only when you ask for them. Progress from real files. Rituals that end on what went well. Privacy by construction. Copy that is honest about the evidence. And your files, in your structure, untouched where you did not touch them.

It is still a folder of plain Markdown. There is still no account, no sync service, no telemetry, and no bundled AI.

We hope it fits.

*The myOS Editors*
