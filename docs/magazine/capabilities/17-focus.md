dek: Focus mode hides everything but the note, on its sheet, on the canvas.
how: ⌘. for focus mode. Escape leaves it.
caption: Rate limits in focus mode, dark theme. The sheet, its text, and a small Exit focus button.
---

⌘. hides the file list, the tabs, the right panel, and the status bar. What is left is the note on its raised sheet, in the middle of the tinted canvas, and one small **Exit focus** button in the corner. Move the pointer to the top of the window and the tab bar comes back while you need it.

Writing works as it does anywhere else: rendered or source, links, blocks, tasks, and saves that check first. Commands that need a panel, such as committing with ⌘⇧Enter, leave focus mode on their own.

For a lighter version, fold only the sides. ⌘B hides the file list and ⌥⌘B hides the right panel. With both folded you have the tabs and the text. The widths of the text column, Narrow, Normal, and Wide, are in Settings under Editor.
