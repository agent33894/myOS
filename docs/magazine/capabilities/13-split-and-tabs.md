Every note, screen, and view opens in a tab. A single click in the file list opens a preview tab that the next click replaces, so browsing does not fill the tab bar. Double-click, or start typing, and the tab stays.

⌘\ splits the window to the right, and pressing it again goes back to one. Each side has its own tabs and can show any screen: a note on the left and Today on the right, or two notes you are comparing. Drag the line between them to share the width.

⌘1 to ⌘8 go to a tab in the side you are working in, and ⌘9 goes to the last. Ctrl-Tab and Ctrl-Shift-Tab step through them. ⌘W closes a tab. Tabs, the split, and which folders are open in the file list are remembered for each folder, so they come back the next time you open it.
