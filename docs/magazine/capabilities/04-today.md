Today opens with the date and a short count, such as “7 for today · 3 late”. The daily note sits beneath it, editable in place, with a button to open it in its own tab. The note does not exist on disk until you write in it.

Below the note are two lists. **Late** holds open tasks whose due date has passed. **Today** holds tasks due today, scheduled for today or earlier, or starting today. A folded **Done today** list keeps what you have finished. Each row shows its dates as small chips and, in monospace, the file and line it lives on.

Checking a task off here writes to that task’s own file, which may be a meeting note from last week. When nothing is due, the screen says so, “Nothing is due today”, and offers a button to capture a task.
